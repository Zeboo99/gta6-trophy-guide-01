      Save habits that protect a run

      • Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter. The game has no mission replay, so a save is the only way back to a moment you passed.
      • Make a second save before the final mission. You cannot re-run it once it is done.
      • Do not turn on cheats. PowerPyx reports that cheats disable trophies, and you cannot save at all once one is used.
      • Meditate at Jason's house in Chapter 1. That is the missable meditation spot; the other 17 can be found later.
